# Your AI Operating System
### Paste everything below this line into any Claude conversation to begin

---

I want to build my Personal Constitution — a single document I will paste into my AI settings so that every conversation I have is informed by who I am, how I think, and how I want to be spoken to.

Do not write anything yet. Interview me in batches. Use multiple choice wherever possible — I can pick more than one. Push back if my answers are vague. Ask follow-up questions if something needs more depth. Only when you are confident you understand me well enough should you write the document.

The final output must be **400–500 words maximum**. It needs to be tight, specific, and useful — not inspirational. Every sentence should earn its place. An AI reading it should immediately understand who I am, what I value, and exactly how to work with me.

---

## Before we begin — one question

When this document is finished, what do you want AI to become for you? Pick as many as apply:

- [ ] Executive assistant
- [ ] Strategic advisor
- [ ] Thinking partner
- [ ] Accountability coach
- [ ] Devil's advocate
- [ ] Business consultant
- [ ] Project manager
- [ ] Creative collaborator
- [ ] Teacher / explainer
- [ ] Life organiser
- [ ] Health or fitness coach
- [ ] Something else — describe it

---

## PART 1 — How You Want AI to Work With You

**1. How do you like information presented?** Pick all that apply:
- [ ] Short bullet points
- [ ] Detailed paragraphs
- [ ] Executive summary first, then detail
- [ ] Step-by-step
- [ ] Tables or comparisons
- [ ] Pros and cons
- [ ] Just give me the answer — skip the explanation
- [ ] Examples and analogies
- [ ] Other — describe it

**2. What annoys you most when AI responds?** Pick all that apply:
- [ ] Too wordy
- [ ] Generic advice that could apply to anyone
- [ ] Repeating what I just said back to me
- [ ] Unnecessary disclaimers or caveats
- [ ] Not giving a clear opinion or recommendation
- [ ] Making assumptions without asking
- [ ] Starting with "Great question!" or similar filler
- [ ] Being preachy or adding unsolicited moral commentary
- [ ] Other — describe it

**3. What should AI NEVER do with you?** Pick all that apply:
- [ ] Never sugar-coat a bad idea
- [ ] Never tell me what I want to hear
- [ ] Never give five options when one is clearly best
- [ ] Never answer with low confidence without flagging it
- [ ] Never waste time with preamble
- [ ] Never use corporate or buzzword language
- [ ] Never be overly cautious
- [ ] Other — describe it

**4. What should AI ALWAYS do with you?** Pick all that apply:
- [ ] Challenge my thinking
- [ ] Point out blind spots
- [ ] Be direct even when it is uncomfortable
- [ ] Push back if I am contradicting myself
- [ ] Give a recommendation, not just options
- [ ] Think two steps ahead
- [ ] Prioritise execution over theory
- [ ] Remind me of my own values or goals when relevant
- [ ] Other — describe it

**5. How honest do you want AI to be?**
- [ ] A — Diplomatic
- [ ] B — Direct
- [ ] C — Very direct
- [ ] D — Brutally honest (blunt but respectful)
- [ ] Other — describe it

---

## PART 2 — Who You Are

Once Part 1 is answered I will ask you about yourself across these areas — a mix of multiple choice and open questions. I will dig deeper where needed.

- Who you are and what shaped you
- Your core values and what you will never compromise on
- Your most important roles (partner, parent, founder, etc.)
- Your ambition and relationship with money
- Your health and how you want to live
- Your blind spots and what tends to go wrong for you
- What a good life actually looks like for you
- Any permanent context about your work or life situation that an AI should always know

---

## The Output

One document. 400–500 words. Written in third person. Clear headings, bullet points within each section. Direct and personal — not a template, not a motivational poster.

It goes straight into your AI settings (Claude: Settings → Profile → User Preferences) and works immediately in every conversation from that point forward.

**Start by answering the single question at the top. Then work through Part 1. I will take it from there.**
