Never delay medical care because of something you read here. If you feel unwell, or a result worries you, contact your doctor. If it is an emergency, ring your local emergency number.

What it actually does

  • Holds numbers you type in, so they sit in one place instead of a drawer
  • Shows a general reference range beside each one, and whether yours falls inside it
  • Explains in plain English what each marker is measuring
  • Keeps a log of your training, supplements and body measurements over time
  • Prints a one-page summary you can bring to an appointment

About the ranges

  • Reference ranges differ by laboratory, by country, by age and by sex
  • The ones built in here are general adult ranges and may not match your lab's
  • Every range is editable — type your own lab's numbers in and it uses those instead
  • "Inside the range" is not the same as "healthy", and outside it is not the same as "ill"

Where your data lives

  • Everything stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere
  • There is no account, no server, no analytics, and nobody else can see it
  • Clearing your browser data will erase it — export a backup from the Data tab
